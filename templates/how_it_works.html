How It Works

Simple steps to connect customers with trusted local professionals

For Customers

1

Post a Job

Describe your task in detail, set your budget, preferred date and time. It takes just a few minutes to create your job listing.

2

Get Quotes

Workers matching your requirements will submit quotations. Compare prices, reviews, and experience to choose the best fit.

3

Negotiate & Hire

Communicate directly with workers to negotiate terms. Once you agree, assign the job and confirm the schedule.

4

Job Execution

Your assigned worker will arrive at the scheduled time. Track progress and communicate through our messaging system.

5

Payment & Review

Complete payment upon completion of work. Leave a review to help other customers find this reliable professional.

6

Build Favorites

Mark great workers as favorites for quick access. Hire them again for future projects without re-searching.

For Workers

1

Create Profile

Set up your profile with your skills, experience, and hourly rates. Add a professional photo and detailed bio.

2

Set Availability

Manage your schedule by marking dates and time slots when you're available to work. Update it whenever needed.

3

Browse Jobs

View jobs matching your skills and location. See job details, customer ratings, and required qualifications.

4

Submit Quotation

Submit your price quote and any additional details. Negotiate directly with the customer if needed.

5

Get Hired & Work

When your quotation is accepted, the job is confirmed. Arrive on time and complete the work professionally.

6

Earn & Build Rep

Get paid for completed jobs. Build a strong reputation through customer reviews to attract more work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using LocalKaam

How do I post a job?

Simply sign up as a customer, click "Post a Job", fill in the job details including location, date, time, and description. Our system will automatically match you with nearby workers who have the required skills.

How is payment handled?

Payments are processed securely through our platform. Customers pay after the job is completed, and workers receive their earnings directly to their registered payment method within 24-48 hours.

Are workers verified and insured?

All workers must complete profile verification including background checks. We recommend customers review worker ratings, reviews, and certifications before hiring. Verify any required insurance based on your local requirements.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

If you're not satisfied, communicate your concerns with the worker first. If issues persist, contact our support team with details. We have a dispute resolution process to protect both customers and workers.

Can I negotiate pricing?

Yes! You can negotiate directly with workers through our messaging system. Share your budget, ask for discounts, or negotiate terms before confirming the job. Workers have full control over their pricing.

How do I become a worker on LocalKaam?

Sign up as a worker, complete your profile with your skills and experience, set your hourly rates, and mark your availability. Once verified, you can start receiving job offers from customers matching your skills and location.
