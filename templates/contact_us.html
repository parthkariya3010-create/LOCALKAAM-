Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I create an account?
Click the "Get Started" button at the top of the page. Fill in your details, choose whether you're a customer or a professional, and you'll be ready to go. The entire process takes less than 5 minutes.
Is it free to join LocalKaam?
Yes! Registration is completely free for both customers and professionals. We only charge a small commission on completed jobs for professionals. Customers never pay any upfront fees.
How do I post a job as a customer?
After creating an account, go to your dashboard and click "Post a Job". Provide details about what you need, your location, and your budget. Our system will match you with qualified professionals in your area.
How are professionals verified?
All professionals go through our verification process, which includes ID verification, skill assessment, and background checks. We ensure that everyone on our platform is trustworthy and qualified.
How do payments work?
All payments are processed through our secure platform. Customers pay only when satisfied with the work, and professionals receive payment securely. We offer multiple payment methods for your convenience.
What if I'm not satisfied with the service?
We take customer satisfaction seriously. If you're not satisfied, contact our support team immediately. We have a dispute resolution process to ensure fair outcomes for both parties.
